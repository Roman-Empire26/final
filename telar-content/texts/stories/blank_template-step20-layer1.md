---
title: "Learn more"
---

Rhetorica christiana shows how images can become an active part of teaching instead of just decoration. Visual forms can help organize ideas, guide attention, and support memory. This changes the process because the audience is not only reading written words. They are also using images and visual structure to understand the information. The same idea can be communicated in more than one form depending on the audience and purpose. This connects to the fluidity of process because teaching can shift between writing, images, and memory. The tool changes the way knowledge is presented and the way the audience interacts with it.
