title: "Learn more"
---

The Codex Selden changes the experience of reading because the reader has to follow figures, symbols, and directional lines instead of normal written text. The path through the page becomes part of the reading process. Meaning comes from both the images and the order in which they are followed. This makes the experience more active and engaging. The reader has to work through the page rather than simply move across lines of writing. The structure of the codex helps guide the story and gives the page its own rhythm. Because of that, the form of the codex shapes how the reader understands the information. It shows that reading can change depending on the tool being used.
