---
title: "Learn more"
---

Belief can change through the process of encountering, reading, interpreting, and practicing an idea. Rodríguez shows that people can interact with written knowledge in different ways: some simply learn from it, while others examine, compile, or judge the ideas within it. The tool provides access to the idea, but the process of interpretation determines what the person takes from it. **Education → experience → interpretation → practice → changed understanding**.
