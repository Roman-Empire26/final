---
title: "Learn more"
---

Social education gives meaning to the signs and ideas that surround us. Through experience and practice, people learn how to understand these signs collectively. For Rodríguez, education is not just memorizing information; it is a process of learning how to participate in society. What we understand as knowledge is therefore shaped by how we are educated and what we experience. This connects to the idea of the signifier and the signified: a sign can take on meaning because a community develops a shared understanding of what that sign represents. These meanings are not created by the sign alone. They are produced through social experience, education, and repeated practice. This also connects back to the tools used to communicate ideas. A tool can provide a way to represent something, but the meaning of that representation depends on the people who encounter and interpret it. The process is therefore fluid because meaning develops through the relationship between the tool, the individual, and the social environment.
