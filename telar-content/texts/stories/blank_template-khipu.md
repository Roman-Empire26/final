---
title: "Khipu"
---

Material Constraint: Complete absence of ink, flat surfaces, or alphabetic lines. Without knowing the original meaning of the writing, what it means is lost. 

Material Expansion: Knots, through cotton or camelid wool, and variables like spin directions, ply, knot configuration, cord hierarchy, and color coding allow complex administrative and narrative data to be stored and read through touch and spatial arrangement rather than visual scripting. "These were knots in different threads of various colors, which were placed in order, though not always in the same order: sometimes one color came before another, and on other occasions they were reversed. This type of communication was a system of cypher..." Garcilaso de la Vega, p. 329).
