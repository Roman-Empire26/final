The Musica painted manta shows that communication does not have to happen through a written page. Meaning can also be carried through a physical object and the images placed on it. The material, design, and way the object is used all become part of the process. This makes communication more flexible because information can move through different forms depending on the setting and audience. The object is not simply holding the message. It becomes part of the message itself. This connects to the fluidity of process because tools can change form while still helping people create and communicate meaning.
