title: "Learn more"
---

Concrete poetry changes the reading experience because the shape and placement of the words become part of the poem itself. The reader has to pay attention to both the language and the way it is arranged on the page. This makes form part of the meaning instead of something separate from it. The eye may move in a different direction depending on the shape of the poem. That changes the pace of reading and makes the page feel more active. The poem becomes something that has to be looked at as well as read. This shows how the structure of a text can guide the reader and change the way the message is understood. The design of the page becomes part of the reading process. It showed that visual form could change how a reader moved through and understood a text.
