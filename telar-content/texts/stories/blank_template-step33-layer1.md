We can see the story still stays faithful to the dynamic storytelling while loosely using structure to not completely lose the reader. I think this is a nice, simple assist without overly supporting the reader into a numbing state. As structured, words tell you, as books nowadays do. Structure now is telling you: you consume; end of story. Codex Mendoza and this image specifically allow you to be engaged without outright telling you what to feel/understand, and you are not simply consuming/viewing. You are actively a part of the story as you break it apart every step of the way. A simple assist with dotted lines here and there won't ruin the experience and will enhance it, as you are not having to start from scratch every read.
