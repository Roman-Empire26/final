title: "Learn more"
---

The khipu shows that a tool can shape not only how knowledge is stored but also who is able to work with that knowledge. Information was organized through cords, colors, placement, and different kinds of knots. Understanding these choices required experience with the system. This meant that knowledge did not simply exist on the object by itself. It depended on people who knew how to create and interpret it. The process becomes fluid because meaning moves between the physical tool and the person using it. The khipu therefore gives us an example of how tools can create specialized roles around knowledge. A tool can preserve information while also affecting who has access to it and who has the ability to explain what it means.
