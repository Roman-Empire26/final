title: "Learn more"
---

The khipu changes the reading process because it does not rely on a normal page or written words. Meaning comes through the cords, knots, and the way they are arranged. The reader has to understand how those physical details connect. This made the experience depend heavily on people who already knew how to interpret the system. Reading the khipu was not automatic. It required specific knowledge and familiarity with the tool. That makes the process more hands on and more dependent on both the object and the reader. The khipu shows that reading can involve touch, structure, and memory just as much as sight. It showed that reading was less about decoding words and more about knowing how to understand the system itself.
