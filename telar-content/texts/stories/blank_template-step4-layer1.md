Rodríguez describes society as an organic system where different parts work together and influence one another. Education, customs, authority, and citizens are connected rather than existing separately. Education helps create the customs and ways of thinking that shape the social body, while those customs influence how individuals participate within society. Changing one part of this system can therefore affect the others. This idea connects to the role of tools because tools and the processes surrounding them exist within these larger social relationships. The way people communicate, teach, and preserve ideas can influence how knowledge moves through the social body. At the same time, society determines how those tools and forms of communication are understood and used. Rodríguez's composition visually reflects this interconnected process by arranging words and ideas so that their relationships help produce meaning. The tool is therefore not separate from society; it becomes part of the process through which society organizes, communicates, and reproduces ideas.
